# Eliza homepage group-chat surface

- Mode: persuade.
- Audience: mainstream users who should understand Eliza without knowing agent terminology.
- User job: recognize Eliza as the useful member of a real group conversation.
- Primary action: Text Eliza. Secondary actions: Call, Telegram, and Discord.
- Proof: five finite fictional rooms (friends, co-parenting, household, trip, and community) where Eliza follows the current thread, attributes speakers, summarizes settled details, and keeps open decisions visible.
- Direction: preserve the established native iMessage visual world, with recognizable iOS navigation, participant photos, sender grouping, bubbles, cards, and composer. Keep orange limited to Eliza brand and the desktop primary CTA; use neutral iOS grays and blue inside the conversation.
- Responsive contract: desktop and mobile use the same message script and participant data. Desktop presents the phone beside the value proposition; mobile turns the phone into the full-height surface and preserves the whole-screen tap target.
- Truth constraints: bounded in-conversation memory is always available in the illustrative script. Show runtime-backed connected calendars, public web search, room-only memory, and personal reminders only through a neutral source line that makes connection, scope, freshness, or permission visible. Humans talk normally; the source line carries access truth, without setup dialogue such as "connect my calendar." Never imply silent access, universal connector availability, booking, purchasing, or messages sent outside the room.
- Intelligence direction: Eliza should proactively synthesize constraints, surface the best overlap, filter real-world options, assign primary and backup owners, balance work, plan for exceptions, answer follow-up questions from the room history, and use already-approved connected tools when they materially reduce coordination. People should not need to ask Eliza to search, remember, compare, verify, or remind them. Eliza asks only the smallest human decision it cannot resolve. Every private source requires explicit participant consent; every public result names its freshness; durable context stays scoped to the room. Treat allergies as hard constraints and proactively check a venue's current published protocol. Report the exact safeguards found rather than claiming any venue is universally allergy-safe.
- Pacing contract: every room opens with its four-message human setup, Eliza's first smart response, and the matching recap card already visible, so the use case and result are legible immediately. Playback begins with the next human reply; all later human messages use believable reading and typing pauses that vary with message length. Show the active humans' avatars, names, and one compact iOS-style typing bubble during that pause. When replies overlap, stack the avatars in the same row and collapse cleanly to whoever is still typing. Eliza should answer noticeably faster. Hold the completed room long enough to read its closing exchange and final recap before switching.
- Tempo contract: keep the overall rotation brisk enough for a homepage while preserving believable human pauses. A multi-person typing indicator must overlay avatars inside the normal avatar column so its names and typing bubble never jump horizontally when the active typer count changes.
- Sound contract: do not imitate or ship Apple system sounds and do not add per-message audio. Every deliberate tap that opens the contact sheet plays one original synthesized welcome shimmer; it must never autoplay, must never trigger outside that menu-opening action, and must fail silently when audio is blocked.
- Scenario contract: five distinct rooms each tell a 20-message story with four evolving state recaps, including a late constraint that tests whether the plan still holds, then rotate continuously as a complete set. Never repeat one room in place or reuse its cast/photos in another room. Household opens first and is the reduced-motion snapshot; Friends occupies Household's former third slot. Use Household, Co-parenting, Friends, Trip, and Community as the changing iOS group titles; do not add a separate category strip or numeric progress text. Co-parenting uses dry treaty/operations humor between terse exes, while Ava's care stays competent and never becomes the punchline.
- Dialogue contract: human messages should read like ordinary low-effort group texts, not polished product copy. Prefer fragments, contractions, simple words, occasional lowercase, and natural "lol" or "pls" where it fits. Do not use semicolons, operational jargon, or unnaturally clever consensus jokes in human bubbles. Keep Eliza clear and capable so the contrast demonstrates the product.
- Memory copy contract: Eliza should apply established room preferences without making people repeat themselves. Keep provenance subtle in the recap source; never make Eliza say "shared room profile" or make a participant restate a known allergy merely to explain the feature.
- Household contract: roommates report facts, constraints, or complaints; Eliza owns the coordination. It should apply the remembered rotation, count completed work, cap overload, adjust around availability, reuse the saved grocery list and home inventory, and finish with a visibly fair split without waiting for people to self-assign every chore.
- Trip contract: Eliza should reconcile every travel calendar and remembered group preference before people ask. When choosing food, satisfy the vegetarian traveler without accidentally selecting a vegetarian-only place for the traveler who prefers meat; surface one inclusive shortlist rather than making either person restate the preference.
- Reference: current Apple group-conversation conventions for a shared title/photo area and visible participants; this is a marketing simulation, not an exact Messages clone.
