# Valhalla privacy policy

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla. TokenJunction LLC is a private company. It is the parent company and the owner of Valhalla. Publish this page on your site, and set `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` to that https address before you ship real ads.

Valhalla is a phone companion for a trading desk that runs on your computer. The phone does not hold a wallet key and cannot send or withdraw funds.

## What the phone stores

- The desk address and optional access code, on this phone only
- The last status, chat, and error lines, on this phone only
- No wallet seed, secret key, or payment card

## Ads

If ads are on, Google AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and approximate location to show and measure ads. Google's policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

The app asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where GDPR or CCPA requires it. You can turn ads off by building with `ADS_ENABLED=false`.

The app can also show our own messages from a JSON feed we host. Those messages are titles, text, and optional https links. They are not a wallet action.

## Desk connection

The phone sends chat text and setting confirmations to the desk address you enter. The Google AI Studio key stays on the computer.
