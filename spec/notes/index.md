# Notes

The notes package keeps the owner's notes, their recordings and their
Trash. The agent reads only the notes the owner selected, saves only the
draft it was given, and a deletion keeps a restorable copy until the
owner's Trash rules let it go. Recordings stay on the device until their
note's operations say otherwise.
