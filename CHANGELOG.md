# Changelog

## 0.1.0-test

Test build for sideload. This is not a Play Store release.

- ACCELERATION desk on your PC. Read-only until you arm live trading.
- Bot wallet created or imported in the desk. The key is encrypted on that PC.
- Phone app opens on a pairing screen. It still starts when no PC desk is reachable.
- The phone app uses Google AdMob test IDs only. These are not live ad units.
- No send or withdraw control on the desk or the phone.

### Install the test APK

1. Download `valhalla-0.1.0-test.apk` from the GitHub pre-release.
2. On the phone, allow installs from unknown sources for your browser or Files app.
3. Open the APK and install.
4. The first screen is pairing. Leave it blank until your PC desk is running.

### Known limits

- Debug-signed test key. Android will warn that the publisher is unknown.
- Test ads only. They are not paid ads.
- The phone does not hold the wallet key.
- Live swaps stay off until you arm them on the PC.
- Play Store listing, live AdMob IDs, and the public X launch are not in this build.
