# Valhalla privacy policy

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla. TokenJunction LLC is a private company. It is the parent company and the owner of Valhalla. This policy covers the Valhalla phone app and the desk on your computer. Publish this page on your site before you ship paid ads. Set `PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` to that https address.

Valhalla is a companion. The phone watches a desk that runs on your computer. The phone does not hold a wallet key. It cannot send, withdraw, or take custody of funds.

## Data on the phone

The phone stores, on that phone only:

- The desk address and the optional access code
- The last status, chat lines, and error lines
- Counts of how many times a house message was shown or opened

It does not store a wallet seed, a secret key, or a payment card. Delete phone data is on the error screen. That clears this data. There is no Valhalla account on a server, so there is no server copy to delete.

## Ads

If ads are on, Google AdMob may use the advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Google's policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where GDPR or CCPA requires it. Build with `ADS_ENABLED=false` to turn ads off.

House messages are titles, text, and optional https links from a feed you host. The impression and click counts stay on the phone. They are not sent to us with a device id.

## The desk

The phone sends chat text and confirmed setting changes to the desk address you enter. The Google AI Studio key stays on the computer. AdMob report keys, if you set them, stay on the computer and are not shown in the page.

## Contact

TokenJunction LLC is the publisher. This public file does not include a street address or a tax id. The support email stays in the private store listing.
