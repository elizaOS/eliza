## Full description

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla Desk Companion. It shows the status of a Valhalla desk that runs on your computer.

The phone does not hold a wallet key. It cannot send, withdraw, or take custody of funds. If you trade, you arm that on the computer.

You can:

- Pair with the desk on your network, or scan the desk address QR
- See the last known status when the desk is offline
- Ask the same agent that runs on the computer
- Confirm a settings idea before it is saved
- Ask the computer to restart its loop only when no position is open
- Read a local error log and delete the data stored on the phone

Ads may appear on the pairing screen and the error log. They do not cover the recover control. The app is for adults.
