# Valhalla brand

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla. The mark is a gold V with three candlesticks in the gap. Use the SVG files in this folder. Do not redraw the mark.

## Colors

- Gold light: `#E8B65A`
- Gold deep: `#C9963E`
- The gold runs from light at the top to deep at the bottom.
- Near black: `#07080B`
- Ice glow: `#9FD4FF` at low strength, only along the inner edge
- Paper white for the mono mark: `#F4F1EA`

## Files

- `mark.svg` is the mark alone on a transparent background.
- `icon.svg` is the mark on the rounded near-black square.
- `lockup.svg` is the mark plus the word VALHALLA.
- `mark-mono.svg` is the same mark in one flat light color, for dark surfaces.
- `png/` holds the raster exports.
- `valhalla.ico` is the Windows app icon.
- `telegram-avatar.png` is the square TJ uploads in BotFather.
- `png/github-social-1280x640.png` is the image for the GitHub social preview.
- `png/icon-512.png` is the Play store icon.
- `png/play-feature-1024x500.png` is the Play feature graphic.
- `png/og-1200x630.jpg` is the site share image.

## Clear space

Leave empty space around the mark equal to one quarter of the mark height. Do not crop the candlesticks or the point of the V. On a small icon, keep the rounded square background.

## Do

- Use the gold gradient on near black.
- Use the mono mark when the gold gradient will not print.
- Keep the word VALHALLA in the lockup. Do not change the spelling.

## Do not

- Do not rotate, outline, or add a drop shadow other than the ice edge.
- Do not place the mark on a busy photo.
- Do not recolor the gold to the old amber `#FFB347`.
- Do not add a profit claim, a wallet address, or a street address next to the mark.
