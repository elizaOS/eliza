Valhalla privacy policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

TokenJunction LLC publishes Valhalla. TokenJunction LLC owns Valhalla. This policy covers the Valhalla website, the Pro web portal, the Android, iOS, and Windows apps, the Chrome extension, the Telegram bot, and the Brain API.

This policy is not financial advice. Valhalla does not promise profit. Crypto is risky. You can lose money.

This page does not include an EIN or a street address.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

These are the Limited Use statements for user data. They apply to the Chrome extension and to every other Valhalla app in this policy.

  • We do not sell user data.
  • We do not transfer user data to third parties except as needed to provide the service, or as required by law. The named processors are Lemon Squeezy for payments, Google for sign-in and AdMob, Cloudflare for hosting, and Solana RPC providers for public balances.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the item's single purpose.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • Humans do not read user data except with your consent, for security, or when the law requires it.

The single purpose of the Chrome extension is to show read-only status from your Valhalla desk on the same computer. The single purpose of the other Valhalla apps is to show that same read-only desk, your public wallet data, and your plan. We do not use the data for any other purpose.

What we collect

We collect only what the service needs:

  • Wallet public addresses that you connect or type. A public address is not a private key.
  • Email and name from Google sign-in, if you choose Google. We request basic profile and email only. We do not request access to your Gmail inbox.
  • Settings and watchlists that you save.
  • A license key, or the end of that key, so we can turn Pro on. We do not collect your card number. Lemon Squeezy processes the payment.
  • Usage and diagnostics, such as whether a request failed and how many API calls a key made.
  • On the free plan only, an advertising identifier through Google AdMob, and only after the consent screen. Pro has no ads.

What we never collect

  • Private keys
  • Seed phrases
  • Transaction approvals, or any signature that would send or withdraw funds
  • Browsing history
  • The content of websites you visit

The apps do not ask a wallet to sign or send a transaction. A login message only proves that you hold the public address. It expires. It does not move funds.

Chrome extension

The Chrome extension stores the desk address and the pairing token in Chrome local storage on that browser. That data stays on the device. The extension does not send it to TokenJunction. It talks only to the desk on that computer, at 127.0.0.1 or localhost. You can remove the extension at chrome://extensions.

Processors

We use these processors only to provide Valhalla:

  • Lemon Squeezy, for payments and license checks
  • Google, for sign-in and, on the free plan, AdMob
  • Cloudflare, for hosting the Brain API and the portal
  • Solana RPC providers, such as a public RPC or a provider you choose, to read public balances

We do not allow these processors to use the data for their own purposes. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Ads

The free plan can show ads. Personalized ads run only after you consent. Pro has no ads. Ads are not shown on wallet connect, login, billing, or settings. A full-screen ad does not run when the app opens.

The Android and iOS apps use Google AdMob test units until a real publisher unit is set. The Windows desk and the web portal can show a Google AdSense banner, client ca-pub-6504655762357230, only on the free plan and only after consent. The Chrome extension does not load AdSense or AdMob. It shows a small house banner stored in the extension package. That banner is labeled Ad or Sponsored. A link may be a Valhalla offer, a community link, or a disclosed affiliate. The extension does not send that click to an ad network script.

Google's policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Cookies from ads are used only to show and measure the ad. You can withdraw consent. Pro removes these ads.

How long we keep data

We keep account data while the account is open. We keep a license record while the plan is active and for a short time after, so we can restore a purchase. Local data on a phone, a computer, or in the extension stays until you delete it. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security.

Delete and export

You can export your account data and delete the account in the Pro portal. You can delete phone data in the app. You can remove the extension in Chrome. You can also email hello@valhalla-app.xyz and ask us to delete or export the account data we hold. We will not ask you for a seed phrase to do that.

GDPR and CCPA

If the GDPR or the CCPA applies to you, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. You can object to a use or ask us to limit it. You can withdraw consent for ads. You will not receive a worse price for using a privacy right. To make a request, email hello@valhalla-app.xyz. You may also complain to your local authority.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Children

Valhalla is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

We store license keys and API keys as hashes where the service allows it. Session cookies are HttpOnly. We do not put private keys on a phone or in the extension. No method is perfect. Please use a current browser and device.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new text on this page and change the effective date. If a change is material, we will also post a notice on https://valhalla-app.xyz.
