---
title: Plugin contribution policy
description: elizaOS focuses on maintained first-party capabilities and no longer accepts third-party plugins or registry items.
---

The community plugin registry is retired. elizaOS no longer accepts third-party
plugins, new registry listings, listing updates, or registry submission tooling.
Related issues and pull requests are closed as out of scope.

Contributions focus on the first-party runtime, applications, and maintained
integrations. Read the
[contribution policy](https://github.com/elizaOS/eliza/blob/develop/CONTRIBUTING.md)
before proposing work.
