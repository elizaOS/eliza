Support

Help with any Eliza Research product: the Eliza app for desktop and mobile, the elizaOS framework, Eliza Cloud, and the Eliza browser extensions.

Contact us

Email support

support@elizaresearch.ai

We read every message and aim to respond within two business days. Include the product, your platform and version, and what you expected to happen versus what happened.

Bug reports and feature requests

Eliza is developed in the open. Search existing issues or open a new one at github.com/elizaOS/eliza/issues. Public issues are the fastest path for reproducible bugs; use email for anything involving your account or private data.

Security vulnerabilities

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately through GitHub Security Advisories rather than a public issue. We acknowledge reports and coordinate disclosure.

Account and data

  • Manage or cancel an Eliza Cloud subscription from account settings in the app or the Eliza Cloud dashboard.
  • Delete your account and its data as described in the privacy policy, or email privacy@elizaresearch.ai.

Documentation

Developer and user documentation lives at docs.elizaos.ai.
