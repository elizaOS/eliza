# Eliza Research

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating Eliza Research, open-source contributors, and prospective
collaborators who need to understand what the company makes and how to take
part.

## Product Purpose

Eliza Research builds products at the intersection of artificial intelligence
and the people who live with it. The site makes that thesis legible, introduces
Eliza and slop.cash, and gives visitors a direct path to the open-source work or
the team.

## Positioning

The company treats agency—not raw intelligence—as the scarce capability:
software should act for people, answer to them, and remain open to everyone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is static HTML, CSS, and browser JavaScript with no framework or
  build step.
- Eliza and slop.cash are the only products described by the current approved
  Research copy.
- Contact is a displayed/copyable email plus verified GitHub and X destinations.
  The preview does not claim the email route is deliverable, and there is no
  contact-form backend.
- Team-member profiles, legal terms, a privacy policy, customer claims,
  benchmarks, and commercial terms have not been supplied and must not be
  invented.
- The particle mark must respect reduced motion, pause when hidden or
  offscreen, remain responsive, and keep one shared implementation.

## Brand Commitments

- Write **Eliza Research**, **elizaOS**, **Eliza**, and **slop.cash** exactly.
- Preserve the canonical Eliza mark and Shaw's orange `#ff5800`.
- The user explicitly chose NubsCarson/reflow as the interaction and layout
  reference: dense one-color particle dots, fixed rail or compact mobile bar,
  edge contact chip, and sparse full-viewport compositions.
- The user wants three complete local editions to compare before one is chosen;
  comparison controls must not appear inside the editions themselves.
- Every comparison route is `noindex` and deliberately absent from production
  navigation. Direct local URLs are the review boundary.

## Product Principles

- Put people and agency before model hype.
- Show real products and source code instead of generic claims.
- Build in the open and make participation obvious.
- Keep the experience distinctive without compromising access, truth, or
  performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction fundamentals. Support keyboard
navigation, 44-pixel touch targets, visible focus, reduced motion, short mobile
landscapes, and explicit success and failure feedback.
